Product overview

LivRen-G is a veterinary oral solution formulated to support healthy liver and kidney function in poultry and livestock. It helps protect the liver and kidneys from damage caused by toxins, drug residues, metabolic stress, feed contaminants, and disease-related weakness.

LivRen-G is suitable for use during stress periods, recovery stages, and conditions where liver and kidney support is required.

Indications and recommended uses

Liver and kidney protection

Supports liver and kidney function against damage caused by toxic substances such as Aspergillus flavus decomposition products, drug metabolites, and the body's own metabolic waste.

Liver disorder support

Helps during symptoms related to liver degeneration, hepatomegaly, liver discoloration, fragile liver condition, and yellow-green liver caused by toxin exposure or medicine overdose.

Anti-toxin support

Assists the body in reducing the harmful effects of toxins and supports natural detoxification functions.

Growth and performance support

Use during 18–20 days and 26–28 days of age may support body resistance, improve feed conversion efficiency, and help reduce FCR.

Recovery support

Helps improve body function and promotes recovery after disease, stress, medication, or toxin exposure.
