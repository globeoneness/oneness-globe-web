## How to use this pack
1. **Clips:** paste each shot's **English prompt** into an AI video tool (Google Veo, Kling, Runway or Sora). Generate it at **9:16, 1080p**, at the length shown. Generate 2–3 versions and keep the best.
   - Keep the global style line (below) at the start of every prompt so all clips match.
   - **Pack shots (3A, 6B, 7A):** use **image-to-video** with the original pack photo as the reference image, so the design is not changed.
   - Never ask the tool to write Bengali text. Subtitles are added in editing.
2. **Voice:** a **human Bangladeshi male narrator** records the Bengali narration below (one file per scene, WAV or high-quality MP3).
3. **Assembly:** send the clips and the voice files to Claude. Claude cuts them, syncs them to the voice, adds Noto Sans Bengali subtitles and light documentary sound, and exports the MP4.

## Narrator notes
- **Voice and tone:** a mature male voice, calm and confident, like a veterinary specialist teaching, not selling. Medium pace with natural pauses at commas.
- **Pronunciation:**
  - EssenGuard Plus = এসেনগার্ড প্লাস
  - Thymol = থাইমল
  - Carvacrol = কারভাক্রল
  - Cinnamaldehyde = সিনাম্যালডিহাইড
  - Eugenol = ইউজেনল
  - Salmonella = সালমোনেলা
  - Clostridium = ক্লস্ট্রিডিয়াম
- **Recording:**
  - quiet room, phone or microphone 15–20 cm from the mouth, no music
  - one file per scene, 1–2 seconds of silence at the start and end
  - WAV format, or MP3 at 192 kbps or higher

## Compliance (checked)
- Wording is limited to "supports" and "can help". No mortality, resistance or clinical-result claims are shown.
- No instant bacterial elimination: every mechanism shot is gradual.
- Not presented as a replacement for treatment. Closing veterinary guidance is included.
- The leaflet's "no contribution to antimicrobial resistance" and "reduces mortality" claims are **left out on purpose**. Using them in the video would need supporting evidence.
